Support

Something broken, something confusing, or something that should not have happened — write in. You do not have to tell us who you are.

Get in touch

General questions go to the issue tracker. It is public, and it needs a GitHub account — so use it for “how does this work”, never for anything you would not post under a name.

Ask on GitHub

Before you write

Do not include anything that identifies you — not your real name, your employer, your email at work, or a screenshot with any of those in it. Support messages are read by a person and stored like any other mail. Anon is built so we cannot work out who you are, and a support message is the easiest way to undo that by hand.

Your Surrogate username is safe to send. It is already public.

What helps us fix it

  • Which room you were in, and roughly when
  • Your Surrogate username, if the problem is about a specific face
  • What you expected, and what happened instead
  • Whether you were blocked by a rule — a refused vote, a locked thread, or a mute — and which one

A blocked vote or a locked thread is usually Anon working as intended: one human gets one ballot per poll and one voice per thread. If that is what you hit, say so and we will explain rather than treat it as a bug.

Reporting a security problem

If you have found a way to link a Surrogate back to a person, cast a second ballot, escape a mute, or read something you should not be able to, please report it privately rather than opening a public issue:

Report a vulnerability on GitHub

What to expect

Anon is an independent prototype maintained by one person. Replies are best effort and may take several days. If the app is down or a rule is misfiring for everyone, that gets looked at first.
