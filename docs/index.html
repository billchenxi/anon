Anon

Prove you’re human, not who you are.

A World Mini App for the conversations you cannot have under your own name. World ID verifies the person once. A Surrogate is the face they wear in a room — and the room only ever sees the face.

Open in World App Read the source

The problem

Anonymity is not the scarce resource.

Burner accounts are free. What is scarce is anonymity a room can trust — where the reader knows a distinct real person is speaking, and the speaker knows that is all the reader learns.

Ask about your bonus, your rating, or your manager. Nobody learns your name. Everybody knows you are one real person, speaking once.

How it holds up

Three rules, all keyed on the person — never the username.

Per poll

One human, one vote

A second Surrogate from the same person cannot recast a ballot.

Per thread

One human, one voice

Nobody becomes a crowd agreeing with itself.

Per room

Mutes follow the human

A fresh face does not clear a consequence the person earned.

The honest part

What the server can still see.

A privacy app that only lists what it does not collect is asking to be taken on faith. Anon shows you the real number instead.

Your World ID nullifier Never written — used once, then discarded
Ballots Keyed per poll, so they cannot become a history
Linked Surrogates grouped under you A real link
Sealed Surrogates Key held on your device — not groupable at all

A sealed Surrogate keeps its private key on one phone and signs every action. The server stores only a public key and cannot tell that face from a stranger’s. The cost is stated plainly in the app: lose the phone, lose the face.
